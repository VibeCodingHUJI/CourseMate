\# Project Overview

\## Problem

Students struggle to make informed course registration decisions and rely mainly on the Shnaton, which provides dry and limited information.  

There is a lack of high-quality, experience-based insights about university courses.



\## Solution

A website that allows students to:

\- Get course recommendations

\- Read and share course reviews

\- Access real student experiences before and during registration periods





\## User Modes



\## Guest Mode (Open Access)

For users who are NOT yet students at the Hebrew University.



\### Features:

\- Course information browsing

\- Access to lecturer information

\- Course combination forum:

&#x20; - Each faculty/department has a dedicated forum

&#x20; - Users can ask questions about course combinations between majors

&#x20; - Questions are answered by students with relevant experience

\- View existing discussions and answers



\## Registered Mode (Students Only)

For verified Hebrew University students.



\### Features:

\- Access to structured course information by department

\- Full access to course data

\- Access to learning materials:

&#x20; - Lecture summaries

&#x20; - Past exams

&#x20; - Formula sheets and study aids

\- Ability to:

&#x20; - Write course reviews

&#x20; - Rate courses

&#x20; - Contribute to forums and answer questions
